## Verification

Use synthetic profile/read data and the real V10.8 client queue wrapper. The focused checks cover populated and partial routine values, visible safety and medication exceptions, unknown/missing/error/identity-conflict states, unchanged-render disclosure preservation, rejected and confirmed saves, read-only refresh recovery, plus an actual offline `save_belongings` queue receipt. The queue case verifies the stored mutation ID/payload and exact profile identity, preserved draft, safe Back to Guests navigation, no second queue entry after reopening or simulated in-memory draft loss, no shared-save claim and unchanged profile-read count. Queue replay against Apps Script, server acknowledgement of other-device visibility and behavior on a physical mobile device are outside this synthetic test.

node_modules/.bin/playwright test tests/care-profile-exceptions.browser.spec.js
