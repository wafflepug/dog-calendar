Daily arrivals and checkout

Today’s arrivals

  1. Open Care → Staying. Choose Next arrival to focus the next visible pending arrival’s existing Check In button.
  2. Review the intake indicator; use Details → Review care if needed. Intake complete does not confirm all safety checks.
  3. Choose Check In and wait for the verified save.

Share Owner Care Link

  1. Open the dog’s profile. Choose Owner Care Link near the top.
  2. Prepare, then Copy message or Share. Send it yourself in your preferred app.
  3. Link ready means a link exists, not that the owner submitted it. Form received confirms the current digital form. Saved care means current completion is unverified.
  4. Refresh Care after submission. Records & forms and PDF uploads remain available.

Checkout due today

  1. Choose Check Out on the correct guest. It may be under More actions.
  2. Check identity and dates. Review loaded belongings and medication reference. If information is Not loaded, cancel and review Care as needed.
  3. Add an optional checkout handover note, then Confirm checkout.
  4. Wait for success. Only a verified save moves the stay to Past.

Cancel makes no change. A safe connection retry retains the note. Conflicts, queued or uncertain results keep the stay active; follow the error guidance and review the booking. Early Checkout keeps its existing separate review.

The shortcut and sheet reuse loaded information. They do not load every profile or send owner messages automatically.
