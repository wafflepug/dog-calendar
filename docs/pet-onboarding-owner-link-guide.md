# Add a pet and share the care form

The usual flow is **Add stay → Copy owner care link → Send in your usual owner chat**. The app prepares the link and message; you choose who receives it.

## A dog staying for the first time

1. Open **Add → Boarding**.
2. Enter the dog’s name, breed, check-in and check-out dates, owner’s name and contact number. Add a booking note only if useful.
3. Select **Create Booking** once. Wait for the saved confirmation.
4. Choose **Copy owner care link**. If **Prepare owner care link** appears instead, select it first. If neither action is available, open Owner Care Link in Care; do not recreate the booking.
5. Open your usual conversation with the owner, paste the link, check the recipient and send. Open **Other sharing options** for a prepared message or native Share when available.
6. For another pet with the same owner, choose **Add dog for same owner** after the first stay is confirmed. Review the copied owner, phone and dates, then enter the next dog. For a different owner, choose **Add another dog** to start clean.

Boarding opens on your current page. Closing and reopening an unfinished form keeps its entries for this page session; **Start over** clears it explicitly. Reloading the page can lose an unsaved draft.

You only enter booking basics here. The owner completes the detailed care form using their link.

## A dog who has stayed before

1. Open **Add → Boarding → Choose returning dog**. You can also use **Care → select the existing dog → More tools → Book Again**.
2. Choose the existing dog by **Dog ID, owner and breed**. Two dogs can have the same name, so check the Dog ID before continuing.
3. Enter the new stay dates without waiting for a care-history lookup. Choose **Review saved care** if you want to reuse it; this loads and opens the dated review. Check the completed-source date. Review and edit the saved instructions, then explicitly approve copying them. Editing again clears approval; review and approve again. Without approval, the booking does not copy saved care.
4. Select **Create Confirmed Stay**. When the stay is saved, the owner care-link action appears in the same booking panel.
5. Choose **Copy owner care link**, paste it into the owner’s chat and send. If needed, choose **Prepare owner care link** first. Ask the owner to review saved details and update anything that has changed.

## Multiple visits or several dogs

1. Open **Add → Multiple visits** and enter the shared owner details.
2. On **Dogs**, choose the exact dog, review saved care if wanted, then choose **Add current dog**. Each added dog shows its captured care choice and available source date.
3. Use that dog's **Edit** action to make changes before saving starts. Choose **Update dog** to apply them, or **Cancel** to keep the original. Changing saved instructions requires review again or a deliberate choice to copy no saved care.
4. On **Dates**, check the compact dog list before adding visit dates. Review the complete dog/date schedule on **Review**.
5. Confirm once. If some visits are queued or failed, use the existing Resume action; do not start a replacement plan for successful visits. Editing is unavailable after saving starts.
6. Expand **Share a saved stay’s care form**, choose one saved dog/date result, and check its Dog ID and dates before copying. Paste that stay’s link into the owner’s usual chat and check the recipient before sending. Copying does not send a message. Queued or failed stays have no sharing action. If a saved stay has no returned link, prepare it later from **Care → Owner Care Link**.

## Share the form later, or send it again

1. Open **Care**, select the dog and check their stay dates.
2. Select **Owner Care Link** in the main profile actions.
3. Check the selected dog and dates. Use **Review stay details** if you need to inspect the fields.
4. Select **Prepare owner message**.
5. Choose **Copy message** and send it through the owner’s usual chat. **Copy link** is available if you want to write your own message.

Preparing again with the same details in the current dialog reuses the prepared result. Copying does not send a message. After using Share, check your messaging app to confirm it was sent.

## What the owner does

1. Open the link on their phone or computer.
2. Complete the guided sections: booking and owner; dog information; behaviour and safety; food and walks; health and home; daily care and handling.
3. Review the answers, confirm the declarations, sign and submit.
4. Look for the form’s completion confirmation. You can then review the submitted information in the dog’s Care profile and **Records & forms**.

The existing owner form and its required safety questions remain in place. Owners should review all prefilled information rather than assume it is current.

## If something interrupts the flow

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Saved on this device / awaiting sync | Keep the page available and check sync status. Once the stay appears in Care, use Owner Care Link there. Do not create the same stay again. To enter a different pet, use Add another dog. |
| Save failed | Your entries remain. Check the connection, then use the retry button. |
| Stay saved but no care link returned | For a single booking, choose **Prepare owner care link** in the saved booking result, or open **Care → Owner Care Link**. For a multi-visit plan, prepare the link later from **Care → Owner Care Link**. The booking does not need to be recreated. |
| Copy was blocked | The text is selected. Press and hold to copy on your phone, or use Ctrl+C on a computer; then paste it into the owner’s chat. |
| Sharing cancelled | Nothing is marked as delivered. The message remains ready to copy or share. |
| More than one Dog ID has the same name | Stop and verify the correct record. The app blocks the current name/date link action when it cannot safely distinguish the dogs; do not rename or merge dogs just to bypass this check. |

## Quick check before sending

Confirm **the right owner, the right dog and the right dates**. Send each pet’s own link to its owner; do not publish owner form links.
