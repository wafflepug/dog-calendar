# Add a pet and share the care form

The usual flow is **Add stay → Copy message → Send in your usual owner chat**. The app prepares the link and message; you choose who receives it.

## A dog staying for the first time

1. Open **Add → Boarding**.
2. Enter the dog’s name, breed, check-in and check-out dates, owner’s name and contact number. Add a booking note only if useful.
3. Select **Create Booking** once. Wait for the saved confirmation.
4. Under **Owner message ready**, choose **Copy message**. This includes the dog, stay dates and their care-form link.
5. Open your usual conversation with the owner, paste the message, check the recipient and send. On supported phones, **Share** can open your messaging app instead.
6. If adding another pet, choose **Add another dog** after the first stay is saved.

You only enter booking basics here. The owner completes the detailed care form using their link.

## A dog who has stayed before

1. Open **Add → Boarding → Choose returning dog**. You can also use **Care → select the existing dog → More tools → Booking tools → Book Again**.
2. Choose the existing dog by **Dog ID, owner and breed**. Two dogs can have the same name, so check the Dog ID before continuing.
3. Enter the new stay dates. Review any saved care details before choosing to reuse them.
4. Select **Create Confirmed Stay**. When the stay is saved, its owner message appears in the same booking panel.
5. Choose **Copy message**, paste it into the owner’s chat and send. Ask the owner to review saved details and update anything that has changed.

## Share the form later, or send it again

1. Open **Care**, select the dog and check their stay dates.
2. Select **Owner Care Link** in the main profile actions.
3. Check the selected dog and dates. Use **Review stay details** if you need to inspect the fields.
4. Select **Prepare owner message**.
5. Choose **Copy message** and send it through the owner’s usual chat. **Copy link** is available if you want to write your own message.

Preparing again with the same details in the current dialog reuses the prepared result. Copying does not send a message. After using Share, check your messaging app to confirm it was sent.

## What the owner does

1. Open the link on their phone or computer.
2. Complete the guided sections: booking and owner; dog information; behaviour and safety; food and walks; health and home; daily care and handling.
3. Review the answers, confirm the declarations, sign and submit.
4. Look for the form’s completion confirmation. You can then review the submitted information in the dog’s Care profile and **Records & forms**.

The existing owner form and its required safety questions remain in place. Owners should review all prefilled information rather than assume it is current.

## If something interrupts the flow

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Saved on this device / awaiting sync | Keep the page available and check sync status. Once the stay appears in Care, use Owner Care Link there. Do not create the same stay again. |
| Save failed | Your entries remain. Check the connection, then use the retry button. |
| Stay saved but no care link returned | Find the saved stay in Care and choose Owner Care Link. The booking does not need to be recreated. |
| Copy was blocked | The text is selected. Press and hold to copy on your phone, or use Ctrl+C on a computer; then paste it into the owner’s chat. |
| Sharing cancelled | Nothing is marked as delivered. The message remains ready to copy or share. |
| More than one Dog ID has the same name | Stop and verify the correct record. The app blocks the current name/date link action when it cannot safely distinguish the dogs; do not rename or merge dogs just to bypass this check. |

## Quick check before sending

Confirm **the right owner, the right dog and the right dates**. Send each pet’s own link to its owner; do not publish owner form links.
