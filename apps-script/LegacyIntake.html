Legacy Intake Scanner

Upload a new intake or reuse a stored one. Waffle tries the strongest configured reader first and automatically falls back when needed.

OCR priority: 1. Groq → 2. Gemini → 3. Tesseract
Groq Vision is attempted first. If it cannot complete the read, Waffle tries Gemini. Only if both cloud readers fail or are unavailable does the scanner run free Tesseract OCR in this browser. Existing conflict checks and review safeguards still apply.

1. Choose the guest & intake form

When opened from a Care profile, the matching stay is preselected. Stored files can be re-read without uploading them again.

PDF, JPG/JPEG, PNG and WebP are supported, up to 10 MB and 20 PDF pages. Pages are rendered locally so Groq can be attempted first even for older stored PDFs. Tesseract runs only as the final fallback.
